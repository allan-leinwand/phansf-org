311 & District Supervisor

311

San Francisco's 311 service is an invaluable resource for residents and visitors alike. It serves as the city's official call and dispatch center for non-emergency issues, connecting people with city services quickly and efficiently. Whether you need to report a broken streetlight, graffiti, potholes, illegal dumping, or other maintenance issues on city streets and sidewalks, 311 ensures the right department is notified and follows up.

Beyond infrastructure concerns, 311 also plays a critical role in assisting individuals in need. If you see someone experiencing homelessness and want to connect them with resources — such as shelters, mental health support, or food assistance — 311 can help coordinate services with the appropriate city agencies.

To make reporting easier and faster, download the SF311 mobile app! The app allows you to submit reports with just a few taps. You can upload a photo of the issue, and your phone's location tracker will automatically determine the exact address to direct the request to the correct department.

Download the SF311 App

For those who prefer calling, 311 is available 24/7 by dialing 3-1-1 from any phone within San Francisco. You can also reach them at (415) 701-2311 if calling from outside the city.

By using 311, you're helping keep San Francisco clean, safe, and responsive to the needs of its community!
