Membership & Dues

Join PHAN

Your membership matters. Presidio Heights is more than a place to live. It is our community. PHAN is a volunteer-run neighborhood association that brings neighbors together and advocates on issues that affect the neighborhood. PHAN is open to all residents and local businesses, and paid household memberships provide important support for that work.

Join or renew your household membership for $50.

Why become a member?

Be connected: Membership helps PHAN bring neighbors together through the annual all-neighbor meeting, community gatherings, and other neighborhood events. See PHAN events and community activities.

Strengthen the neighborhood: PHAN has supported community improvements and initiatives, including the Presidio Heights Playground and the Presidio Library.

Have a stronger neighborhood voice: PHAN monitors and advocates on issues affecting Presidio Heights, including development projects, transportation, neighborhood safety, and other local initiatives. See our Neighborhood Projects page for current developments we are following.

Stay informed: PHAN shares neighborhood news and issues through its newsletter, email updates, meetings, and events. PHAN also works with local officials, including District 2 Supervisor Stephen Sherrill, when issues affect our community.

Membership: $50 per household

Your paid household membership supports PHAN's community activities and its ability to advocate with City Hall and other decision makers on matters important to Presidio Heights.
