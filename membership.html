Join PHAN for 2026

Your membership matters. Presidio Heights is more than a place to live. It's our community. PHAN advocates for the neighborhood and brings neighbors together. The more paid members we have, the stronger our voice with City Hall and other decision makers.

Join or renew your household membership for $50 and support PHAN throughout 2026.

Membership benefits

Be Connected: We bring neighbors together through the annual meeting and community gatherings.

Enhance the Neighborhood: PHAN has played an active role in local improvements and community initiatives, including the Presidio Heights Playground and Presidio Library Branch.

Stand Together: PHAN advocates on projects and issues important to the neighborhood, including local development proposals.

Stay Informed: Our newsletter, email updates, meetings, and events help residents understand what is happening in the community.
