Become a Paid PHAN Member for 2026!

Your Membership Matters

Presidio Heights is more than just a place to live. It's our community. PHAN advocates for the neighborhood and brings neighbors together. Our efforts are wide ranging and have helped shape our community. The more paid members we have, the more clout we'll have with City Hall and other parties on matters important to all of us.

Join or renew your membership. Pay $50.00 to support our neighborhood association and enjoy benefits throughout 2026!

Membership Benefits

Be Connected: We bring neighbors together to socialize and to spend time with city decision makers and local merchants through our Annual Meeting and our social gatherings.

Enhance the Neighborhood: We've played an active role in local improvements and will continue to do so as they arise. The Presidio Heights Playground and the Presidio Library Branch are some of the community initiatives that PHAN has championed.

Stand Together: Have your voice heard. We've had an impact on projects and issues that are important to the neighborhood, such as the 3333 California Street and 3700 California Street real estate developments and the Presidio Hill School expansion.

Stay Informed: Be in the know. In 2025 we held community meetings with our District 2 Supervisor so neighbors could learn and give timely input on critical issues. Our quarterly newsletter and email updates cover matters of importance and announce neighborhood happenings.
