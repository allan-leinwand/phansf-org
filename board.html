Board of Directors

The Presidio Heights Association of Neighbors (PHAN) Board of Directors is a dedicated group of residents who care deeply about our exceptional neighborhood. Our board includes working professionals, stay-at-home parents, retirees, and others who come together to protect, enhance, and strengthen the fabric of Presidio Heights, home to approximately 800 households.

Presidio Heights is one of San Francisco's most beautiful and historic neighborhoods, known for its tree-lined streets, elegant homes, and proximity to the stunning Presidio National Park — a park offering trails, scenic overlooks, and cultural landmarks. With a strong sense of community, excellent schools, and a vibrant local culture, Presidio Heights is a wonderful place to call home.

As a board, we actively monitor and advocate on key issues affecting our neighborhood, including:

  • The 3333 California Street, 3700 California Street, and 3637-3657 Sacramento Street development projects
  • Traffic & transportation concerns
  • Neighborhood safety & security
  • Other local initiatives that impact our community

We are always looking for passionate and committed residents to join us in shaping the future of Presidio Heights. The board meets approximately eight times per year, and there is no financial commitment to join. Our nominating committee will present a slate of directors for approval at the annual all-neighbor board meeting.

Why join the PHAN Board of Directors?

  • Make a meaningful impact on your community
  • Connect with your neighbors and build lasting relationships
  • Stay informed and engaged with local developments
  • Have fun while giving back!

If you're interested in joining the PHAN Board, we'd love to hear from you! Contact us at info@phansf.org to learn more.
