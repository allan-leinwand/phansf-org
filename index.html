Welcome to Presidio Heights

Nestled just south of the world-famous Presidio National Park and the spectacular Presidio Golf Course, Presidio Heights is a serene and picturesque neighborhood that offers a peaceful retreat while keeping you just moments away from San Francisco's most scenic parks and hiking trails. This charming community strikes the perfect balance between tranquility and convenience, providing a quiet escape from the city's hustle without feeling removed from its energy.

Home to over 800 residences, Presidio Heights features an elegant mix of single-family homes, apartment buildings, and condominiums, all contributing to its timeless and classic character. Since the 1960s, the Presidio Heights Association of Neighbors (PHAN) has played a vital role in preserving the neighborhood's charm and fostering a strong sense of community. Open to all residents and local businesses, PHAN continues to uphold the welcoming spirit that makes this neighborhood so special.

Guided by a dedicated board of directors, PHAN actively engages in neighborhood matters, from transportation and quality of life initiatives to new real estate developments. The association hosts an annual all-neighbor board meeting, inviting everyone in Presidio Heights to come together, stay informed, and contribute to the community's future.

All neighbors in Presidio Heights are encouraged to JOIN PHAN and be a vital part of your community!

If you would like more information about PHAN, please reach out to us at info@phansf.org.
