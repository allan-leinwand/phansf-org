Welcome to Presidio Heights

A prestigious, serene neighborhood at the edge of San Francisco's most beautiful parks, connected by neighbors who care deeply about its future.

The Neighborhood

A quiet corner of San Francisco, thoughtfully connected.

Nestled just south of the world-famous Presidio National Park and the spectacular Presidio Golf Course, Presidio Heights is a serene and picturesque neighborhood that offers a peaceful retreat while keeping you just moments away from San Francisco's most scenic parks and hiking trails.

Tree-lined streets, distinguished architecture, and a walkable village atmosphere give the neighborhood its unmistakable sense of place.

Sacramento Street brings shops, cafés, and daily errands within an easy walk, while nearby parks and trails keep nature close at hand. It is a quiet escape from the city's bustle that remains connected to its culture, landscape, and energy.

Broad sidewalks and leafy corners invite neighbors outdoors and give each block a gracious, lived-in quality.

A Distinctive Community

Timeless character, sustained by active neighbors.

Home to over 800 residences, Presidio Heights features an elegant mix of single-family homes, apartment buildings, and condominiums, all contributing to its classic character.

Since the 1960s, the Presidio Heights Association of Neighbors (PHAN) has played a vital role in preserving the neighborhood's charm and fostering a strong sense of community.

The neighborhood's relationship with the Presidio is central to its character. The public 18-hole golf course winds through eucalyptus and Monterey pine, and nearby open spaces offer expansive views toward the bay.

Open to all residents and local businesses, PHAN continues to champion the welcoming, engaged spirit that makes this neighborhood so special.

Neighbors in Action

Stewardship begins on the block we share.

Guided by a dedicated board of directors, PHAN actively engages in neighborhood matters, from transportation and quality-of-life initiatives to new real estate developments.

The association hosts an annual all-neighbor board meeting, inviting everyone in Presidio Heights to come together, stay informed, and contribute to the community's future.

All neighbors are encouraged to join PHAN and take a meaningful part in the life of the neighborhood. For more information, please reach out at info@phansf.org.
