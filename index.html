About PHAN
The neighborhood

Timeless character, close to everything.

Presidio Heights is a residential neighborhood in San Francisco bounded roughly by Arguello Boulevard, Masonic Avenue, Pacific Avenue, and California Street. Tree-lined streets, distinctive architecture, Sacramento Street businesses, and the Presidio give it a strong sense of place.

More than 800 residences call the neighborhood home, alongside local businesses and the parks, trails, and views that shape daily life here.

At the edge of the Presidio, the neighborhood is within easy reach of parks, schools, Muni, Laurel Village, Pacific Heights, and San Francisco's north side.

A distinctive streetscape

A century of architecture, still part of daily life.

Presidio Heights is defined by its architecture as much as its location. Formal residences, mature landscaping, and carefully maintained streets create continuity from block to block.

The neighborhood's architectural story is layered rather than uniform, with Edwardian, Victorian, Tudor, Spanish Revival, Mediterranean, and other early-20th-century influences contributing to its character.

That character is one reason thoughtful development and preservation matter. PHAN works to keep residents informed and engaged as the neighborhood evolves.

Our neighbor: the Presidio

One of San Francisco's great parks is part of daily life.

Presidio Heights sits directly beside the Presidio, a 1,491-acre national park site with miles of trails, recreation, historic places, gardens, beaches, and sweeping views. For residents, the park is not simply a destination nearby. It is part of the neighborhood's everyday landscape, woven into the places we walk, play, and gather. Just beyond our streets, the Presidio Gate opens onto Lovers Lane, one of the park's oldest footpaths, while Presidio Wall Park offers playgrounds, fields, courts, picnic areas, and views across the park. These close connections make the Presidio feel less like a nearby destination and more like an extension of the neighborhood itself.

Presidio Tunnel Tops adds another major community resource, with Golden Gate views, public lawns, picnic areas, trails, the Outpost nature playground, the Field Station, and easy access to Crissy Field.

A neighborhood with roots

From high ground to home.

Presidio Heights grew into a residential neighborhood as San Francisco expanded westward. The higher ground near the Presidio became especially attractive after the 1906 earthquake, and much of the neighborhood's distinctive residential fabric dates from the early decades of the 20th century.

Today, that history is visible in the homes, institutions, streets, and enduring relationship with the Presidio. PHAN continues that story by helping neighbors take an active role in the community's future.
