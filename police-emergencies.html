Police & Emergencies

In an emergency, always dial 911.

For non-emergency police matters, including reporting a past crime or suspicious activity, contact the SFPD Non-Emergency Line at (415) 553-0123.

To provide a crime tip, call the SFPD Tip Line at (415) 668-7387.
