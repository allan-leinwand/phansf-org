2026 Annual Meeting

Held Wednesday, September 2, 2026. The annual meeting brought neighbors together to hear from community and city leaders and discuss issues affecting Presidio Heights.

Featured speakers included Prado Group President and CEO Dan Safier, District 2 Supervisor Stephen Sherrill, and moderator Jan Yanehiro. The meeting was held at the Presidio Golf & Concordia Club.

This event has concluded. Check back for future PHAN meetings and gatherings.
