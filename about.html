About PHAN

Neighbors working together for Presidio Heights.

PHAN is the volunteer neighborhood association for Presidio Heights in San Francisco. We bring neighbors together, advocate on issues that affect the community, share useful information, and help preserve the neighborhood's character.

Presidio Heights is home to more than 800 residences and sits directly beside the Presidio. Its tree-lined streets, distinctive early-20th-century architecture, Sacramento Street businesses, parks, and long-standing institutions create a neighborhood with a strong sense of place.

What PHAN does

Advocate

Represent neighborhood interests on development, transportation, safety, quality-of-life, and other local issues.

Connect

Bring residents together through community meetings, events, and opportunities to engage with local leaders.

Inform

Keep neighbors up to date through newsletters, events, neighborhood projects, and practical city resources.

Our community role

PHAN has roots in Presidio Heights dating to the 1960s and remains open to residents and local businesses. The association is guided by a volunteer board that meets regularly and works on issues affecting the neighborhood's future.

To learn more about current leadership, visit the Board of Directors. To support PHAN's work, join or renew your membership.
