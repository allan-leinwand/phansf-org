Neighborhood Projects

PHAN tracks local development projects that can affect Presidio Heights. This page provides a concise overview and links to project information from the project sponsors.

3637-3657 Sacramento Street

Litke Properties is developing a four-story mixed-use residential project in the heart of the Sacramento Street retail corridor, replacing a medical/office building and parking structure.

Project information

3700 California Street

The 3700 California project will transform the former hospital site into a residential community designed to fit the surrounding neighborhood and support a walkable lifestyle.

Project information

3333 California Street

Presidio Highlands is a proposed housing and mixed-use development at 3333 California Street led by the Prado Group and its development partners.
